# About Me

**Luke Laverton**: Senior Technical Specialist, Identity & Access Management.

I've been working in identity since 2015, and with SailPoint IdentityIQ since 2017.

I established and now run the SailPoint Engineering function at M&G, responsible for automating, enhancing and securing SailPoint IdentityIQ. Alongside my wider team, I also look after all things Entra: enterprise applications and app registrations for SSO, conditional access, privileged identity management, Entra Connect (AADC), and all things users and groups.

This site is where I tell tales of my trials and tribulations, discuss interesting identity topics, and look at the future of identity.

Opinions here are mine.
